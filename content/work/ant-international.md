I joined Ant International as a Backend Engineer Intern, under the Ant Bank HK team. Although my official role was backend engineer intern, my tasks were all full-stack, covering frontend, backend, and QA.

## What I did

Contributed to the **Ant Bank HK digital banking ecosystem**, working on migrating banking workflows, encrypting customer statements, knowledge-base tooling, and internal knowledge retrieval.

I migrated and deployed 2 banking workflows while preserving approval processes, access controls, and downstream integrations with other microservices. On the same system, I implemented automatic PDF encryption for bank-issued statements. By removing manual encryption steps for the Operations team, the process can be error-free and more efficient, thus improving customer satisfaction.

My team had also been working on an **internal knowledge base upkeep pipeline**, where domain and architecture knowledge are periodically updated into a single shared knowledge base. For this, I integrated employee audit logs, source-evidence drift tracking, and troubleshooted pipeline failures. As part of my research to enhance this pipeline, I experimented and built a **local RAG prototype** to improve knowledge retrieval speed and accuracy.

## What I learned

My time at Ant taught me some valuable lessons:

- Collaboration and communication are of utmost importance.
- Human review in the agentic coding era is very much needed.
- Taking ownership of my work, and any mistakes that I made with regards to it, is an admirable trait.
