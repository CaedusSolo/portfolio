Currently, I'm serving as the Vice President of Goal Execution at IT Society MMU, where I am responsible for overseeing the Public Relations and Multimedia divisions. Towards the last 3 months of my tenure, I've also assumed the role of Acting President, in the President's absence.

## Reshaping the Organization
I made the decision to abolish the Marketing division, after observing its lackluster impact from the previous term. I redistributed marketing duties to Multimedia and Public Relations: Multimedia would be in charge of content creation and Public Relations would deal with any external partnerships with other organizations.

## Strengthening the Society's Online Presence
I introduced the initiative of regular blog updates on [our website](https://itsocietymmu.com/blogs) and recap reels on [our Instagram](https://instagram.com/itsocietymmu) as a way to document our events and activities. I wanted our activities to leave a lasting record, beyond the day of the event itself.

## Overseeing Major Events
I served as advisor for Tech Career Days and CodeNection, both of which are IT Society's flagship events. I mainly provided guidance and direction to the committees when they needed it, but I mostly left the execution details to them. 

Tech Career Days was successfully held for the first time since 2023, drawing over 500 students to attend, while CodeNection had nearly 700 sign ups. Getting funding was rather difficult compared to previous years, but we pulled through.

## Outreach and Collaboration
Under my supervision, IT Society organized 4 industrial visits to external companies, in particular, to Jobstreet by SEEK, LGMS Berhad, Firmus Sdn. Bhd, and to Setel Ventures, a slight increase from the 3 visits conducted in the previous term.

Other than that, we partnered with Jobstreet by SEEK and MBOT (Malaysian Board of Technologists) to have tech sharing sessions here in MMU.

## Giving a Full Workshop
In April, I ran a **Tailwind CSS Workshop** as a speaker (read how it went [here](https://itsocietymmu.com/2026/05/01/teaching-you-to-design-smarter-not-harder/)). It was my first time giving a workshop as a speaker, after a lot of convincing by Jordan, the Head of Skills Development. I introduced Tailwind, its use cases, best practices, and a practical follow-along session where I guided the participants to design their own portfolio sites.

## Stepping Into a Larger Role: Acting President
In September, following several months of limited involvement from the President in club matters, I assumed the role of Acting President alongside my existing responsibilities as Vice President.

This was a decision I considered carefully, particularly given my existing workload. Ultimately, I chose to step into the role to provide continuity for the committee and keep the club’s activities moving forward.

## My Takeaways
I learned that camaraderie and teamwork are absolutely crucial in a club's success. The committees that poured in their blood, sweat, and tears are the ones that make running IT Society possible.
