CodeNection 2025 was a national-level hackathon open to all university students in Malaysia. It had a record 897 participants, with 269 teams participating. 

I served as Head of Technical Division. My division was tasked to develop the official CodeNection event website, and handle technical equipment (AV system, microphones, speakers) on the Grand Final.

I credit this experience with contributing to my eventual appointment as Vice President, as it demonstrated my abilities as a capable leader.

## Achievements
- The event website garnered a lot of traction: at its peak it had 4000+ monthly visitors. To date, it has over 10k views.
- Successfully created a modern and responsive website, only using WordPress.
