From December 2024 to December 2025, I was Assistant Head of Technical Division at IT Society MMU. This was the first ever leadership role I've ever held in a club/society.

This role was a major factor in my subsequent promotion to Vice President.

## Building MMU Clubs Site
For the majority of my tenure, the technical team was focused on building MMU Clubs Site, a clubs and societies aggregator for MMU. For the first time, I was given the chance to lead a team in building an actual system. I was quite proud of our accomplishment: we actually managed to deploy a working prototype! 

Although I didn't see this project to its completion in my tenure, the project was handed over to the Technical division of the next term to be continued.

## Organizing MMU Hack Day
I served as the Assistant Director for MMU Hack Day, an internal hackathon for MMU students. It's meant to be a beginner-oriented hackathon to introduce newbies to how hackathons work and what to expect. 

The event was received positively, with about 15 groups signing up (we expected 10). It's something I'm proud to have been a part of, and hopefully it will become one of IT Society's flagship events.
